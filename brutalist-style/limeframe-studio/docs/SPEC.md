# Product Specification

## Goal

Present a digital marketing studio as a sharp, memorable partner for teams that need strategy and execution without agency fog.

## Sections

- **Header:** brand, anchor navigation, consultation CTA.
- **Hero:** positioning statement, primary CTA, abstract campaign illustration.
- **Client strip:** social proof through a compact wordmark row.
- **Services:** four visual cards for SEO, paid media, social, and email.
- **Statement:** a plain-language conversion prompt.
- **Case studies:** three compact proof points.
- **Contact:** high-contrast final CTA.

## Accessibility

- Semantic headings and landmark elements are used throughout.
- Interactive elements have visible focus/hover affordances.
- Decorative artwork is hidden from assistive technology where appropriate.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
