# Design Direction

## Concept: Neon Editorial Brutalism

Limeframe borrows the directness of a printed agency poster and the energy of a creative technology studio. The page uses a pale paper canvas, ink-black panels, and one deliberately loud lime accent.

## Visual language

- `Bodoni Moda` gives the headlines a fashion-editorial silhouette.
- `DM Mono` keeps navigation and supporting copy technical and measured.
- Hard borders and offset shadows create physical, printed-object weight.
- Hand-drawn orbit forms are made with CSS so the artwork stays lightweight.
- Lime highlight bars act like editorial annotations, not decoration.

## Interaction

- Reveal transitions are subtle and staggered to preserve the poster-like rhythm.
- Service cards lift with a hard offset shadow on hover.
- Reduced-motion users receive the complete layout without movement.
